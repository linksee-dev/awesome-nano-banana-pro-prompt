## 🎨 What is Nano Banana Pro

Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image) is Google DeepMind's new state-of-the-art image generation and editing model, built on Gemini 3 Pro. It uses Gemini's state-of-the-art reasoning and real-world knowledge to visualize information better than ever before.

Nano Banana Pro not only turns text prompts into high‑quality images but also offers powerful image‑to‑image editing—enabling targeted retouching, style transfer, and multi‑image blending.

### Core Features

- 🧠 **Enhanced Reasoning & Knowledge**: Leverages Gemini 3's advanced reasoning to generate more accurate, context-rich visuals based on enhanced reasoning, world knowledge and real-time information
- ✍️ **Multilingual Text Rendering**: Generate accurate, legible text in multiple languages within images, perfect for mockups, posters, and international content
- 🎨 **Multi-Image Blending**: Blend up to 14 images while maintaining consistency and resemblance of up to 5 people, easily turning sketches into products or blueprints into photorealistic 3D structures
- 🖼️ **Studio-Quality Creative Controls**: Advanced creative controls including localized editing, camera angle adjustments, focus control, sophisticated color grading, and scene lighting transformations (e.g., day to night, bokeh effects)
- 📐 **High-Resolution Output**: Support for multiple aspect ratios with 2K and 4K resolution options, ready for any platform from social media to print
- ⚡️ **Consistency by Design**: Ensure seamless and consistent branding across every touchpoint

### Use Cases

- 📊 Visualize data as infographics
- 📝 Turn handwritten notes into diagrams
- 🎬 Create prototypes and concept designs
- 🎨 Produce high-fidelity visuals and branded content
- 🌍 Generate multilingual international content
